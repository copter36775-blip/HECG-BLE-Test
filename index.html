CONFIG1 · Conversion

Single-shot halts continuous acquisition after one conversion. This build limits BLE acquisition to 1000 SPS; 2/4/8 kSPS are rejected by the firmware.

CONFIG2 · Reference, clock & test

Test MUX (0x5) uses CONFIG2.INT_TEST. Amplitude is ±VREF/2400. These are signal-chain checks, not IEC calibration signals.

CH1SET · Respiration channel

RESP_DEMOD_EN1 / RESP_MOD_EN are staged OFF automatically while CH1 uses a diagnostic MUX, then the previous RESP1 value is restored when CH1 returns to Normal.

CH2SET · ECG channel

LOFF / LOFF_SENS

The CJMCU preset senses CH2P/CH2N only, because CH1 respiration is AC-coupled in the ProtoCentral circuit.

RLD_SENS

LOFF_STAT.RLD_STAT is the only direct hardware indication. The CJMCU bias network (and JP3) can hold that comparator inside its window even with a floating electrode, so amber SUSPECT means the RLD connection cannot be proven — it is never reported as a false green.
